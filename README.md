# awesome-IELTS Resources

A collection of useful IELTS materials, tools, tips, strategies, practice tests, and applications.

Whether you are just starting your IELTS preparation or preparing for your final exam, this repository brings together useful resources for **Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, Band Scores, and Mock Tests** in one place.

The goal is to make IELTS preparation easier by helping students find useful resources without having to search through hundreds of websites.

## What Can You Find Here?

- 🎯 Band score explanations and IELTS information
- 🎧 Listening lessons and practice tests
- 📖 Reading strategies, question types, and practice tests
- ✍️ Writing lessons, topics, vocabulary, and sample essays
- 🗣️ Speaking topics, vocabulary, and cue cards
- 📝 IELTS Academic and General Training mock tests
- 📚 Additional IELTS preparation resources
- 🌐 Official IELTS preparation resources

## How Can This Help You?

This collection can help you:

- Find IELTS study materials in one place.
- Practice each IELTS skill separately.
- Understand different IELTS question types.
- Improve your Listening and Reading performance.
- Practice Writing Task 1 and Task 2.
- Prepare for Speaking Parts 1, 2, and 3.
- Take mock tests to measure your progress.
- Find additional resources when you need more practice.
- Prepare for both IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training.

You do not need to use every resource listed here. Choose the resources that match your current level, target band score, and weakest skills.

Contributions are always welcome!
